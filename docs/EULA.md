## 4. The server is the sole authority

**Your device is not authoritative, and no local change grants you anything.** Entitlements,
plan limits, credit balances, quotas, permissions and feature access are decided **by the
server on every request**, from the server's own records. The client applications display
what the server reports and hold no authority of their own.

This has consequences you should understand before you buy anything:

- Editing a client application, its local database, or a token it holds does not change what
  the server permits. It produces a client that disagrees with the server, which fails.
- A plan is attached to your **account**, not to a device. Signing in on a new device grants
  the new device what the account has — and nothing more.
- We may correct a client that has been made to display otherwise, and we may adjust a
  balance that was created by a defect rather than by a purchase.
